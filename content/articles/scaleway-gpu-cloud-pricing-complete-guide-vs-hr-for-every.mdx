## Scaleway GPU Pricing Overview

Scaleway is a European cloud provider with GPUs at competitive rates. Mid-range globally, excellent in Europe.

EU data residency? Scaleway makes sense. GPU instances are newer than AWS but supply is growing. This covers pricing and optimization.

### Who Scaleway Works For

- European users (compliance)
- Budget-conscious teams
- Privacy-first developers
- EU infrastructure requirements

Smaller than the big clouds but growing fast. Reliability has improved. Multi-language support.

## GPU Instance Pricing

### H100 GPU Instances

**Single H100 (80GB):**
- Hourly: ~$2.80/hr
- Monthly: ~$1,904/month (18% discount vs hourly)
- Memory: 128GB RAM
- vCPUs: 32 cores
- Storage: 200GB SSD

Scaleway H100 pricing is competitive. $2.80/hr is significantly better than AWS (~$12.25/GPU/hr on p5 instances) and Vultr ($4+/hr).

**Cost comparison:**
- Scaleway: $2.80/hr
- RunPod: $2.69/hr (similar)
- AWS p5 H100: ~$12.25/GPU/hr (8-GPU p5.48xlarge ~$98/hr total, 4x more)
- Vast.AI: $2.00-3.50/hr (variable)

Scaleway is competitive globally for H100.

### A100 GPU Instances

**Single A100 (40GB):**
- Hourly: ~$0.89/hr
- Monthly: ~$605/month
- Memory: 64GB RAM
- vCPUs: 16 cores
- Storage: 100GB SSD

A100 pricing at $0.89/hr is exceptional for a managed EU cloud provider.

**Cost comparison:**
- Scaleway: $0.89/hr
- Google Cloud: ~$2.93/hr (3x more)
- RunPod: $1.39/hr (56% more)
- DigitalOcean: $2.68/hr (3x more)
- Vast.AI: $0.80-1.50/hr (variable, less reliable)

Scaleway A100 is outstanding value among managed cloud providers.

### L4 GPU Instances

**Single L4 (24GB):**
- Hourly: ~$0.42/hr
- Monthly: ~$286/month
- Memory: 32GB RAM
- vCPUs: 8 cores

Inference GPU at budget price. Excellent value for small inference projects.

**Cost comparison:**
- Scaleway: $0.42/hr
- Vast.AI: $0.12-0.20/hr (cheaper but less reliable)
- Google Cloud g2: $0.35/hr
- AWS g4dn: ~$0.35/hr

Scaleway L4 is reasonable though Vast.AI is cheaper.

### Multi-GPU Instances

Scaleway offers configurations with 2-4 GPUs:

**Dual H100:**
- Hourly: ~$5.60/hr (2x single)
- Monthly: ~$3,808/month

**Quad A100:**
- Hourly: ~$3.56/hr (4x single)
- Monthly: ~$2,420/month

Pricing scales linearly. No bulk discount.

## Regional Pricing

Scaleway has data centers in Europe, North America, and Asia:

**European regions (Paris, Amsterdam):**
- Base pricing (most competitive)

**North American regions:**
- +10-15% vs Europe

**Asia-Pacific regions:**
- +20-25% vs Europe

Europe is cheapest. Scaleway's strength in EU market is reflected in pricing.

For global teams, EU data centers offer better pricing than US options. Privacy-conscious projects benefit.

## Pricing Models

### Hourly Billing

Pay per hour, stop anytime. No commitment required. Most flexible.

### Monthly Commitment

18-20% discount for monthly commitment. Instance is "reserved" for the month. Early termination loses remainder.

**A100 example:**
- Hourly: $0.89/hr = $649.70/month (730 hours)
- Monthly: $605/month
- Savings: $44.70/month (7%)

Monthly is better for continuous workloads.

### Annual Commitment

Multi-year commitments are less common on Scaleway than competitors. Inquire for custom pricing on large orders.

## Real-World Pricing Examples

**Small inference project (10 hours/week):**
- Scaleway L4: 10 × $0.42 = $4.20/week
- Vast.AI L4: 10 × $0.15 = $1.50/week
- AWS g4dn: 10 × $0.35 = $3.50/week

Scaleway is mid-range. Vast.AI cheaper, AWS similar.

**Medium training (100 hours/month):**
- Scaleway A100: 100 × $0.89 = $89/month
- Vast.AI A100: 100 × $1.00 = $100/month (variable)
- Google Cloud A100: 100 × $2.93 = $293/month
- RunPod A100: 100 × $1.39 = $139/month

Scaleway is very competitive. RunPod and Vast.AI are also affordable alternatives. Google Cloud is significantly more expensive for managed A100.

**Heavy training (24/7 for 3 months):**
- Scaleway A100: 730 × $0.89 × 3 = $1,815
- Scaleway (monthly): $605 × 3 = $1,815
- Vast.AI (average): $730 × 3 = $2,190
- Google Cloud A100: ~$2,139/month × 3 = $6,417
- AWS p4: ~$1,825/month × 3 = $5,475

Scaleway is an excellent value for longer commitments, significantly undercutting both AWS and Google Cloud for A100 workloads.

## Cost Optimization Strategies

### 1. Use monthly commitment

7-10% discount for monthly billing. Worth it for continuous workloads.

### 2. Right-size the instance

L4 at $0.42/hr for inference. A100 at $0.89/hr for training. Pick based on actual needs.

### 3. Use European pricing

If teams are in Europe, Scaleway's pricing is best in region. EU data residency is a bonus.

### 4. Batch processing

Run multiple jobs sequentially. Reduces startup overhead.

### 5. Stop instances when idle

Stopped instances don't charge for compute. Storage charges continue but are minimal.

### 6. Monitor consumption

Scaleway's dashboard is transparent. Set alerts for budget overruns.

### 7. Consider multi-month commitments

For projects running 3+ months, bulk pricing might be available. Inquire with sales.

## Storage and Bandwidth

**Block Storage:** $0.06/GB/month (cheaper than most competitors)

**Data Transfer:**
- Outbound: $0.10/GB (higher than some providers)
- Between Scaleway zones: Free
- Inbound: Free

Keep data in Scaleway. Transfer costs add up for large datasets.

## European Data Residency Advantage

Scaleway's key differentiator is EU data centers. For teams requiring:
- GDPR compliance
- EU data residency
- European infrastructure isolation

Scaleway is natural choice. Cost is secondary to compliance.

German teams especially benefit from Scaleway's strong EU presence.

## Reliability and Support

Scaleway offers:
- 99.9% SLA for Compute instances
- Email support (free) and priority support (paid)
- Growing documentation
- Community forums

Support is less mature than AWS/Azure but adequate. SLA is solid.

Uptime is generally good. Less documentation than major clouds but improving.

## Integration with Scaleway Ecosystem

Scaleway offers complementary services:

- **Object Storage:** S3-compatible, $0.015/GB/month
- **Managed Databases:** PostgreSQL, MySQL
- **Container Registry:** Docker image hosting
- **Managed Kubernetes:** Container orchestration

Integration is good for teams already using Scaleway infrastructure.

## Limitations

**Smaller ecosystem:** Fewer pre-built integrations vs AWS/GCP

**Regional spread:** Primarily EU. Limited US/Asia options.

**Newer platform:** Less mature than decade-old competitors

**Documentation:** Good but less extensive than major clouds

**Support:** Adequate but not 24/7 premium support like AWS

These limitations are acceptable for many workloads. For bleeding-edge deployments, major clouds are safer.

## When to Use Scaleway

**Use Scaleway for:**
- European teams (best EU pricing)
- GDPR/data residency requirements
- Cost-conscious budgets
- A100 inference/training
- Teams already on Scaleway platform
- Projects willing to trade support for cost savings

**Avoid Scaleway for:**
- Mission-critical production (use AWS/GCP)
- latest hardware requirements (older batches)
- 24/7 premium support needs
- Complex multi-region deployments
- Highly regulated industries requiring extensive audits

Scaleway is excellent for European teams with standard ML needs and moderate budgets.

## FAQ

**Is Scaleway cheaper than AWS?**

For A100, yes — Scaleway at $0.89/hr versus AWS at ~$2.93/hr is significantly cheaper. For H100, Scaleway at $2.80/hr is also far more affordable than AWS's ~$12.25/GPU/hr on p5 instances. AWS has more production features and global coverage.

**Should I use Scaleway or Vast.AI?**

Vast.AI is cheaper but less reliable. Scaleway is more reliable but more expensive. Choice depends on risk tolerance.

**Can I run production ML on Scaleway?**

Yes. SLA is 99.9%. Reliability is solid. Suitable for production.

**What GPU should I start with on Scaleway?**

L4 at $0.42/hr for inference/learning. A100 at $0.89/hr for training. H100 at ~$2.80/hr for heavy lifting.

**Does Scaleway support PyTorch?**

Yes. All major ML frameworks work. Scaleway provides Linux instances; you install your tools.

**Is data stored in Scaleway encrypted?**

Storage can be encrypted. Check security settings during instance creation.

**Can I scale horizontally on Scaleway?**

Yes. Launch multiple instances. Orchestrate with Kubernetes or manually. Multi-GPU instances available.

**How do I minimize my Scaleway bill?**

Use monthly commitment. Right-size instances. Stop when idle. Use L4 for small jobs.
