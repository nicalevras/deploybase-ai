## Cheapest H100 Europe: H100 Pricing Overview

H100 in Europe: $2.70-$4.50/hr. Stabilized in March 2026. Europe costs 10-20% more than US due to supply and infra.

PCIe cheaper than SXM (simpler wiring). SXM better for multi-GPU work, costs more.

Commitment discounts: monthly saves 10-20%, annual saves 25-40%.

## European Provider Ranking

Nebius ranks lowest among confirmed EU-native H100 providers at $2.95 per hour. Data centers in Finland and the Netherlands provide reliable EU availability. GDPR compliance is native. This represents best European pricing with included residency benefits. Scaleway does not currently offer H100 GPU instances.

Nebius operates H100 infrastructure in Finland and the Netherlands. H100 pricing starts at $2.95 per hour with GDPR-compliant EU data residency. Nebius offers competitive pricing for teams requiring EU-native hosting and monthly commitment discounts of 15-25%.

OVHcloud provides H100 access from French and German data centers. Pricing starts at approximately $4.10 per hour on-demand. OVHcloud's sovereign cloud offering ensures data remains within EU jurisdiction, appealing to enterprises with strict data residency requirements.

CoreWeave operates in Amsterdam serving EMEA. Eight H100s cost $49.24 per hour, translating to $6.15 per GPU. This premium pricing reflects premium support and global reach. Higher cost justifies investment for mission-critical workloads.

AWS operates regional data centers in Ireland, Frankfurt, and Milan. H100 pricing is approximately $12.25 per GPU per hour (p5.48xlarge at ~$98/hr for 8 GPUs; full instance only, no single-GPU option). European regions may add 5-15% premium. Reserved instances reduce costs roughly 50% with one-year commitments (~$49/hr).

Google Cloud operates H100s in Belgium and Germany. H100 pricing on A3 instances is approximately $3.30/GPU/hr (~$26/hr for 8 GPUs). Google's Vertex AI integration provides value for managed ML. Committed discounts available.

Azure provides H100 access through European data centers. NC H100 v5 instances (single and 2-GPU) start at approximately $6.50/hr per GPU. Reserved instances reduce costs 30-50%. Existing Azure customers benefit from commitment consolidation.

RunPod lacks dedicated European presence, routing traffic through global data centers. H100 SXM costs $2.69 per hour globally. Network latency from North American data centers reaches 100-150 milliseconds to European users. This latency might be acceptable for batch workloads.

Lambda Labs offers H100 SXM at $3.78 per hour with global infrastructure. European latency is higher than local providers. Their reliability guarantees may justify the cost for production workloads despite geographic limitations.

## Additional Cost Factors

Bandwidth costs add 15-20% to monthly bills. European providers typically charge EUR 0.01-0.02 per GB egress. Internal transfers between data centers are free. Teams with high data movement should optimize data flow carefully.

Storage incurs additional costs. Persistent volumes cost EUR 0.08-0.10 per GB-month. A 1TB checkpoint costs $8-10 monthly. Teams maintaining multiple model versions face mounting storage costs.

Support and SLA commitments add cost. Managed support with 1-hour response times costs $200-500 monthly. Most teams accept slower support for development workloads.

Regional taxes vary by jurisdiction. German VAT at 19% applies to Frankfurt. French VAT at 20% applies to Paris. Tax-inclusive pricing should be verified before final budgeting.

## Total Cost Optimization

Batch processing benefits from spot pricing. European spot instances cost 40-50% less than on-demand. Workloads tolerating 5-10 minute interruptions should prioritize spot capacity. Monthly spot costs for eight H100s might reach just $500 versus $2,200 on-demand.

Reserved instance commitments reduce effective hourly costs. One-year AWS H100 commitment reduces hourly rate to $2.50. Teams operating H100s continuously should maximize commitment discounts.

Multi-month Nebius commitments provide alternatives. Nebius monthly commitments offer 15-25% discounts. Annual commitments can reduce effective H100 costs to approximately $2.20-2.50 per hour.

Geographic distribution spreads workloads efficiently. Distributing training across multiple regions avoids single-region capacity constraints. This increases complexity but enables handling larger workloads.
