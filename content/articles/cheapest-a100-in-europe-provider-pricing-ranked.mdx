## Performance Benchmarks

A100 performance remains consistent across providers when network latency is controlled. Memory bandwidth of 2TB/s enables efficient multi-GPU training across European data centers. Most European providers achieve sub-50ms latency within regional boundaries.

Inference workloads see similar performance metrics regardless of location. Training jobs benefit from direct connections between GPUs, making intra-data-center placement crucial for European deployments.

## Regional Factors

European data center selection involves GDPR compliance considerations. The Netherlands and Germany offer clear data residency guarantees. France's data sovereignty requirements may limit some provider options.

Bandwidth costs vary by country. German facilities typically charge lower egress fees than UK providers. Cross-border data transfer within the EU faces fewer restrictions than US-Europe transfers.

Power availability and cooling infrastructure in Scandinavian facilities sometimes offer lower rates due to abundant hydroelectric power. Iceland-based options provide additional cost advantages but include higher latency for EU-based clients.

## FAQ

**Where is the cheapest A100 hourly rate in Europe?**
RunPod offers the lowest rates at $1.19 per hour for A100 PCIe globally, including European regions, as of March 2026. Among EU-native providers with GDPR-compliant infrastructure, Nebius ($1.60/hr) and Scaleway ($1.75/hr) offer the best rates.

**Do prices vary by specific European country?**
Yes, significantly. Netherlands and Germany offer the lowest rates, while London and Paris facilities command 15-25% premiums due to higher demand and power costs.

**What's the difference between A100 PCIe and SXM?**
A100 SXM offers superior memory bandwidth and is optimized for multi-GPU training. The $0.20 hourly premium reflects this performance advantage. For single-GPU inference, PCIe provides better value.

**Are there discounts for monthly commitments?**
Most providers offer 10-20% discounts for monthly reservations. RunPod and Lambda both provide commitment-based pricing that reduces effective hourly rates.

**How do European A100 prices compare to US pricing?**
European rates are typically 5-15% higher due to power costs and lower competition in some regions. US providers like [aws-gpu-pricing](/articles/aws-vs-azure-gpu-pricing) often undercut European alternatives for commodity GPU types.
